The house at the end of the lane kept a lamp in the front window. Each evening the lamp was lit before the street grew dark. Neighbors used it as a marker when the fog came in from the river. A child walking home counted the gates until the light appeared.

The wick was trimmed every Sunday and the glass was wiped clean. No one remembered who had started the custom. They only knew the window should not go dark. On the night of the first frost, the lamp burned until morning.
